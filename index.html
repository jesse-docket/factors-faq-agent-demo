Frequently asked questions

What does Factors help marketers do?

Factors brings together account engagement, intent and attribution to help teams understand which accounts to prioritize and how marketing contributes to pipeline.

Which resource should I start with?

For account discovery, explore Buyer Intent Data. For competitive creative research, try LinkedIn Advisor. For methods and examples, browse the webinar library.

Can I explore without sharing my email?

Yes. These resource summaries and sample previews are available without an email gate.

Can Factors Guide show me where to look?

Ask the agent about your objective or a specific topic. When a relevant resource matches, it can guide you to the page and explain the useful sections.

Does this site generate reports or play webinars?

No. This demonstration contains written session summaries and illustrative tool previews. It does not submit forms, send reports or play recordings.
