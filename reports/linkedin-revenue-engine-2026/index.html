LinkedIn benchmarks / Report

From Benchmarks to Blueprints: The LinkedIn Revenue Engine for B2B

Factors research team

Demonstration replica · Source page date: 2026-09-24

Overview

Benchmark research across 50,000 closed deals, 850+ B2B companies and $150M+ in LinkedIn ad spend, covering channel mix, revenue quality, buying-committee coverage and the length of the research window.

Key takeaways

  1. LinkedIn's share of B2B paid spend grew from 31% to 39.8% in 18 months.
  2. LinkedIn returns 1.6x ROAS against Google's 1.18x, and LinkedIn-sourced deals close at 34% higher ACV.
  3. Reaching six or more contacts before a deal opens lifts win rates by 17 points; three or more during the active cycle adds 16.
  4. Buyer research starts about 124 days before a deal appears in CRM.

When this helps

LinkedIn ROAS, channel mix and budget share, paid benchmarks, proving pipeline contribution to leadership, buying-committee coverage, how early buyer research starts.

Try asking: “Is LinkedIn actually worth it, and how do I prove it to my CMO?”

Who this is for

CMOs, VP Marketing, MarketingOps, Demand Gen, Account-Based Marketers and RevOps
