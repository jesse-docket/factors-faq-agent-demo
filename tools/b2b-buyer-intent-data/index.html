Overview

Explore how category-level research signals can help prioritize an account list. Factors’ live tool offers a list of companies showing interest in your category. This demonstration shows an illustrative output, not actual buying activity.

Key takeaways

  1. Category research can help prioritize account outreach.
  2. Company-level intent is different from identifying a person on your website.
  3. Use intent alongside account fit and other engagement evidence.

When this helps

In-market accounts, Bombora, category research, target-account prioritization, outbound lists.

Try asking: “Which accounts are researching our category?”
