Overview

See how a competitor comparison can inform your next LinkedIn creative test. Factors’ live tool compares three competitors’ messaging, pain points, recurring phrases and content formats. This demonstration uses fictional examples.

Key takeaways

  1. Compare the pain points and value propositions competitors emphasize.
  2. Look for recurring messages and creative formats.
  3. Turn a differentiated angle into a testable creative hypothesis.

When this helps
