Developer marketing / Session summary

Orchestrating the Full Funnel: Developer Marketing in 2026

James Sarris, Cockroach Labs

Event: 2026-08-27 · Source page date: 2026-09-03

Overview

Developer marketing must serve the person testing the product and the executive approving its use. This conversation explores behavioral targeting, technical content and the connection between self-serve evaluation and enterprise buying.

Key takeaways

  1. Identify technical evaluators through product and CRM behavior.
  2. Connect technical detail to the business risks executives evaluate.
  3. Measure useful engagement and downstream outcomes rather than form fills alone.

When this helps

Developer audiences, technical evaluators, executive buyers, DevRel, PLG, gated vs ungated content.

Try asking: “How do we reach developers and their executive buyers?”

Who this is for

Teams marketing to developers and technical buying committees
