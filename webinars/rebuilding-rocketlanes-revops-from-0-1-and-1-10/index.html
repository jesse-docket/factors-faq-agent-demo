RevOps / Session summary

Rebuilding Rocketlane's RevOps from 0-1 and 1-10

Dhiraj Kumar, Rocketlane

Event: 2026-09-02 · Source page date: 2026-09-07

Overview

Rocketlane’s approach starts with reliable systems, then aligns metrics with revenue, and only then layers on operational programs. The session explores when shared data infrastructure and automation can remove repetitive work.

Key takeaways

  1. Audit whether CRM stages reflect the actual sales process.
  2. Align metrics and reporting around revenue decisions.
  3. Compare automation and shared data infrastructure with adding manual reporting work.

When this helps

Scaling RevOps, CRM alignment, tool investment, data warehousing, Slack agents.

Try asking: “Our CRM and reporting are fragmented. Where should RevOps start?”

Who this is for

Revenue operations and GTM leaders
