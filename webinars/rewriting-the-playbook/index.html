AI GTM / Session summary

Rewriting the playbook: How Vis Runs AI-Native GTM at Concirrus

Viswanathan Nadarajah, Concirrus

Event: 2026-06-18 · Source page date: 2026-06-23

Overview

This session follows how Concirrus connects account signals, enrichment and outreach across a lean GTM stack. It also illustrates why multi-touch account journeys matter when deciding which channels contribute to a deal.

Key takeaways

  1. Connect account signals with enrichment and coordinated action.
  2. Examine engagement across stakeholders rather than one isolated click.
  3. Use the complete account journey to discuss channel contribution.

When this helps

Lean GTM stack, intent orchestration, Factors MCP/Claude, Clay/HubSpot, multi-touch attribution.

Try asking: “How can a small team connect intent signals to GTM action?”
