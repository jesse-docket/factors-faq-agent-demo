LinkedIn ads / Session summary

Running LinkedIn Ads in 2026

Joshua Stout, Beyond the Funnel

Event: 2026-07-23 · Source page date: 2026-09-09

Overview

A practical discussion of LinkedIn campaign decisions across the funnel. Explore creative formats, lead-generation approaches and ways to connect campaign engagement with pipeline rather than relying on the final click.

Key takeaways

  1. Choose formats and offers for the stage of the buyer journey.
  2. Review targeting and budget allocation together.
  3. Assess pipeline contribution beyond last-click conversions.

When this helps

Full-funnel LinkedIn, document/thought-leader ads, lead-gen forms, budget waste, 1:1 ABM, attribution.

Try asking: “How should we improve our LinkedIn campaign strategy?”

Who this is for

B2B paid media and demand-generation teams
