Enterprise ABM / Session summary

The ABM Playbook Behind High-ACV Deals

Alexander Goodwin, Fingerprint

Event: 2026-08-05 · Source page date: 2026-08-06

Overview

Explore the account planning behind a move toward larger enterprise deals. The session considers strategic-account selection, personalized campaigns and the coordination needed to engage a buying committee.

Key takeaways

  1. Choose account tiers before deciding campaign effort.
  2. Coordinate personalized ads, pages and sales activity around an account plan.
  3. Consider the full buying committee and expansion opportunity.

When this helps

Enterprise/high-ACV deals, strategic accounts, PLG to sales-led growth, personalized pages, committees.

Try asking: “How do we build ABM for high-value enterprise accounts?”

Who this is for

Enterprise demand-generation and account marketing teams
