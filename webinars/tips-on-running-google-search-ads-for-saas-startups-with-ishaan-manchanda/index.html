Google Search / Archive session summary

Tips on Running Google Search Ads for SaaS Startups with Ishaan Manchanda

Ishaan Manchanda, GrowthSpree

Original event date unverified · Source page date: 2026-06-16

Overview

An archival conversation about aligning paid search with buyer intent. It covers campaign readiness, keyword choices, credible landing pages and experimentation aimed at improving lead quality.

Key takeaways

  1. Start with a clear audience and evidence of product-market fit.
  2. Match keywords and ad promises to the landing-page experience.
  3. Evaluate lead quality and test copy rather than relying on bounce rate alone.

When this helps

SaaS search-ad planning, keyword intent, landing-page fit, unqualified paid traffic.

Try asking: “How can our SaaS search ads attract more qualified leads?”

Who this is for

SaaS teams planning or improving paid search
