Understanding Remote Selling with Rachel Shi

Rachel Shi

Original event date unverified · Source page date: 2026-02-09

Overview

An archival conversation about helping buyers make progress between seller meetings. It covers focused educational content, shared deal resources and careful interpretation of account engagement.

Key takeaways

  1. Equip buyers with concise material they can share internally.
  2. Use a deal room to organize relevant resources.
  3. Interpret intent in context rather than assuming every visit signals purchase readiness.

When this helps

Buyer enablement between calls, deal rooms, sales video, interpreting intent signals.

Try asking: “How can we keep buyers engaged between sales calls?”

Who this is for

Sales and marketing teams supporting remote buyers
